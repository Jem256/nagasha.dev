KnowledgeBit is a small quiz platform for learning Bitcoin and Lightning concepts, with Lightning payments used both as an entry mechanic for some quiz rooms and as a reward for high scorers. It started as a way to make BitDevs Kampala's reading material more approachable for newcomers — a quiz you can take after a session instead of a wall of text to re-read.

## Status

Early and evolving. The core quiz engine and LNURL-based payment flow work; the content library (questions, difficulty tiers, topic packs) is the part that grows over time, mostly pulled from BitDevs Kampala sessions and my own blog posts.
