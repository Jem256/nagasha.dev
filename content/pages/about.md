I'm Jemimah Nagasha, a Bitcoin open-source developer in Kampala, Uganda. I work full-time on Bitcoin and Lightning tooling, supported by a [Btrust](https://btrust.tech) Starter Grant. My main project is [Polar](https://github.com/jamaljsr/polar), a desktop app for running local Lightning Network environments.

I started out as a civil engineer. [Here's how I moved into Bitcoin open source](https://blog.btrust.tech/how-curiosity-led-me-from-civil-engineering-to-bitcoin-open-source-contribution/).

Now I help others make the same move. I'm a faculty member of the [Btrust Builders Program](https://www.btrust.tech/builders), where I support learners across the pathways, and I organise [BitDevs Kampala](https://www.bitdevskla.org/), a monthly meetup for discussing and contributing to the research and development of Bitcoin and related protocols.

Outside of code, I love to read everything fiction.
