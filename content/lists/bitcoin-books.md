---
title: Bitcoin books worth your time
description: What I'd actually recommend, not just what's popular.
date: 2025-10-01
tags: [bitcoin, career]
items:
  - label: "Mastering Bitcoin — Andreas Antonopoulos"
    url: https://github.com/bitcoinbook/bitcoinbook
    note: Still the right starting point for the technical layer.
  - label: "The Bitcoin Standard — Saifedean Ammous"
    note: Read for the monetary history argument, not as an engineering reference.
  - label: "Grokking Bitcoin — Kalle Rosenbaum"
    note: Underrated — good diagrams, gentle but not shallow.
  - label: "Mastering the Lightning Network — Antonopoulos, Osuntokun, Pickhardt"
    url: https://github.com/lnbook/lnbook
---

If you only read one, make it *Mastering Bitcoin* — it's the one I reach for again when I've forgotten some UTXO or script detail.
